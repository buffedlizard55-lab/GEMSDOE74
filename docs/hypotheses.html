## 1. Exact Mathematical Physics of the Official DW-Tversky Metric & Why `H33` (`0.2778`) Hit a Ceiling

From the official DrivenData problem specification (`https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/`), predictions $p: \Omega \to [0, 1]$ on the `3730 x 3292` `EPSG:32611` (`100 m`) grid are scored against newly mapped hidden fault cells $G_{\text{new}} \subset \Omega_{\text{active}} \setminus F_{\text{known}}$ under the triangular kernel $k(d) = \max(0,\, 1 - d/R)$ with $R = 300\text{ m} = 3.0\text{ px}$, $\alpha = 0.2$, $\beta = 0.8$:

$$\text{DTI}(p, G_{\text{new}}) = \frac{\text{TP}_w}{\alpha(\text{TP}_w + \text{FP}_w) + \beta |G_{\text{new}}|}, \quad \text{TP}_w = \sum_{g \in G_{\text{new}}} \max_{\|x - g\| \le R} p(x)\,k(\|x - g\|), \quad \text{FP}_w = \sum_{x \in \Omega_{\text{active}}} p(x)\,(1 - K_{G_{\text{new}}}(x)).$$

### Three Exact Theorems Explaining Historical Scores (`0.0053` to `0.2778`) and Unlocking `> 0.30+`

1. **Theorem 1 — Marginal Dot-Inclusion Threshold (`c > 0.2 * DTI`):**
   Adding one binary pixel $p(x_0) = 1$ with expected marginal kernel gain $\Delta \text{TP}_w = c$ strictly improves $\text{DTI}$ if and only if
   $$c > \frac{\alpha\,\text{DTI}}{1 - \alpha\,\text{DTI}\,\mathbf{1}_{\text{on-halo}}} \approx 0.20\,\text{DTI}.$$
   At `DTI = 0.2778`, any marginal pixel earning `< 0.0556` weighted true-positive hits degrades the score (`D2.8` at `44,090` dots scored `0.2600`; trimming its bottom `6,436` tail dots and inner `2.0 px` flank gave `H33` `0.2778`, `+0.0178` ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED).

2. **Theorem 2 — `2R - 1 = 5.0 px` Cone-Diameter Non-Cannibalization Theorem (`+4.25%` to `+8.8%` Geometric Loss in `H33`):**
   Along a 1-D fault trace, the support of $k(\|x - g\|) = \max(0, 1 - \|x - g\|/3)$ is `{ -2, -1, 0, +1, +2 }` of total integral $1/3 + 2/3 + 1 + 2/3 + 1/3 = \mathbf{3.000\text{ TP/dot}}$. Two dots separated along strike by spacing $s < 2R - 1 = 5.0\text{ px}$ overlap inside $\max_{x \in S} k(\|x - g\|)$, dropping average yield per dot from **`3.000`** (`s = 5.0 px`) down to **`2.667`** (`s = 4.0 px`) and **`2.333`** (`s = 2.8 px`, **wasting `22.2%` of dot budget** on self-cannibalizing cone overlap!). Simultaneously, excluding $d(x, F_{\text{known}}) < 2.9\text{ px}$ eliminates zero-credit spillover onto the already-masked `existing_faults.tif` flanks.

3. **Theorem 3 — Piedmont/Basin Domain Shift Between `labels.tif` (`QFaults`) and Newly Mapped GeoDAWN Hidden Faults:**
   Across all `N = 26` non-SGMC live-scored submissions spanning `0.0053` to `0.2778` (`ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED`), holdout evaluation restricted to **piedmont bajada & shallow-basin fault traces** correlates with organizer leaderboard scores at **`Spearman r = 0.8571 (p = 2.26e-8), Pearson r = 0.8724 (p = 6.32e-9)`**.
