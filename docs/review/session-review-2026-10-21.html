Why `h33-h33-2-b2` is not causally explained by the score claim

The official problem page defines a distance-weighted Tversky score with α=0.2, β=0.8 and a linear triangular kernel with 300 m support. This makes sparse predictions with high expected proximity to hidden faults a plausible strategy: low-credit predicted mass contributes false-positive cost, while missing truth contributes false-negative cost. That is a metric-based rationale, not proof of why a particular raster scored as reported.
