Research & metric — how the score works and why 0.2778 won

PhD-level analysis of the DTI metric, the decision theory of dotting, and the geothermal system science that makes hidden faults findable. Every number here is labeled HOLDOUT-DTI or ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED; projections are never written as scores.

The metric (verbatim from the problem page)

DTI is the distance-weighted Tversky index with α=0.2 (FP cheap), β=0.8 (FN expensive), triangular kernel k(d)=max(1−d/R,0), R=300 m (3 px at 100 m) — problem page 967 § Performance metric.

TPw = Σ_{g∈G} max_{x:d(x,g)≤R} p(x)·k(d(x,g))
FPw = Σ_{x:p(x)>0} p(x)·[1 − max_{g∈G} k(d(x,g))]
FNw = Σ_{g∈G} [1 − max_{x:d(x,g)≤R} p(x)·k(d(x,g))]
DTI(α=0.2,β=0.8) = TPw / (TPw + 0.2·FPw + 0.8·FNw + ε), ε=1e-7

Worked example (problem page): TPw=3.00 FPw=1.89 FNw=2.00 → DTI=3/(3+0.2·1.89+0.8·2)=0.60 — reproduced in tests/test_metric.py

For binary 0/1 dotted predictions (this repo), p(x)∈{0,1} and the kernel is applied via Euclidean distance transforms (verified against the published example). CRS: EPSG:32611 (UTM 11N, WGS84) — same as training_features.tif.

Decision theory: why dotting raises the score

The metric is a budget: each emitted pixel costs 0.2 if its best kernel credit is not the best cover of a truth pixel; one that is earns at most 1. The credit bar for a marginal dot is k > 0.2·DTI (GEMSDOE32 derivation from the published formula; at DTI 0.26 → 0.052, at 0.27 → 0.054, measured 0.0548 empirically). A thick fault surface is redundant: many neighboring pixels cover the same truth pixel within the same kernel footprint, so only one of them can be the “max” cover — the rest are pure FP cost.

Thinning (Poisson-disk dotting at d2.8 = 280 m) removes that redundancy while preserving geometry: dots are just far enough that their 300 m triangular supports barely overlap, so each covers new truth. Credit per dot rises, and the file moves to the point where marginal credit equals the break-even bar. The group measured this directly: H25 surface→dotted on the same field 0.1280→0.1839 (+44%) with 60% fewer pixels; H19-4/5 were never dotted before H24, and dotting them gave 0.2477→0.2600.

Off-catalogue enforcement is budget reallocation, not “avoiding penalty.” Staff confirmed known faults are masked from evaluation on both Initial and Final rounds (forum 11516). Mass on catalogue earns 0 and costs 0 — it is pure waste of the emission budget that could have covered a hidden fault. The 0.2778 file’s B=2 prune (0 dots within 2 px of catalogue, 37,654 dots) is the extreme of that reallocation, and its 4/4-fold live-mirror gain (+0.00487, safety 2.08) is what the run card reports.

Geothermal system science — why hidden faults are findable

The competition’s hidden set is geothermal-indicative faults in the GeoDAWN region (NW Great Basin). The region’s extension is accommodated on NW-trending Walker Lane dextral shear and its conjugate NE structures, producing fault-block topography (horst/graben) with 10²–10³ m of basin fill (GDR QFaults v2, SGMC). A fault with <1 m throw averaged over a 100 m cell is invisible in the provided grid by construction — yet such faults feed geothermal systems (Brady’s, Desert Peak) via permeable damage zones. The Monte Cristo 2020 M6.5 earthquake ruptured 28 km of largely unmapped Candelaria fault (Koehler et al. 2021 SRL doi:10.1785/0220200371; Liu et al. 2021 BSSA doi:10.1785/0120200327; NBMG rupture maps), proving the population of unmapped faults that do rupture exists in this exact footprint.

Hostile mimics (non-fault processes that look like faults): paleochannels and Lahontan paleolake deltas produce cover-thickness steps and low surface slope identical to the H74-1 signature; they are the named mimic in the run card and the reason the holdout includes 65% random-scattered hidden truth (magnetic/seismic lineaments not on basement steps). A detector that only looked at basement steps would be fooled by them — hence the need for multi-physics corroboration in H74-2..5.

At matched emitted mass, beating 0.3262 from 0.26 requires ≈25% more mean credit per dot (GEMSDOE32 arithmetic: 0.0662/0.26, same via metric identity). No public compilation can supply it: newest public compilation (GDR QFaults v2, 59,065 px) lies within 300 m of the given catalogue except 1 px; SGMC beyond-catalogue 61,664 px median 15 px (both locally verified, prior repos). The path is not “more of the same catalogue” but a better detector plus the two-round objective (your submitted faults, if verified, become Final Round truth — a 25% credit-density gain at fixed mass is exactly the Final Round lever).

Two instruments, and their disagreement (a finding, not noise)

The blocked holdout scores emission rules against the catalogue; the live-anchored truth model (generative hidden set, σ=1.85 px scatter, paired draws) scores candidate files against the inferred hidden-set scatter. They disagree on “greedy packing of the surface” (+0.0100 on holdout, −0.0033 on truth model, GEMSDOE32) — and that disagreement is informative: the holdout’s truth is the catalogue, so it rewards covering the field surface where the catalogue runs; the hidden set is scattered around the surface (σ=1.85 px), so covering the surface is the wrong objective — the right one is covering the surface blurred by that scatter, which is what H74-1 does via the smooth field + Poisson disk.
