⚠️ The portal error “Predicted values must be in range [0,1]” — diagnosed and fixed
The error fires for two distinct reasons, both measured on this competition’s own data (GEMSDOE30, 2026-10-03):
  • Mechanism 1 — float32 sentinel: training_features.tif uses -3.4028234663852886e+38 as nodata on 7,113,308 cells, of which 3,061 lie inside the submission footprint. Un-sanitized, those cells ship as ≈−3.4×10³⁸, outside [0,1].

What this is

GEMSDOE74 is an auditable system for the DOE GEMS Prize (DrivenData #306, GeoDAWN/NW Nevada): find geothermal-indicative faults not in the USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue and ship them as a legal GeoTIFF. Three parts: exact metric re-implementation and its decision theory; preregistered spatially-blocked holdout scoring emission rules; Bayesian slot gate. Sources are pinned and hash-verified; every number is labeled HOLDOUT-DTI (evaluator version, withheld positives, 95% CI) or ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED (copied from a submission-page receipt). A projection is never written as a score.

Why did h33-h33-2-b2 (0.2778) score highest in the group — and can we beat it?

The metric is a budget

DTI = TPw / (TPw + 0.2·FPw + 0.8·FNw), triangular kernel R=300 m, α=0.2 β=0.8 (problem page 967). Every emitted pixel costs 0.2 if its best kernel credit is not the cover of a truth pixel; one that is earns at most 1. The credit bar for a marginal dot is k > 0.2·DTI (≈0.054 at DTI≈0.27, 0.052 from 0.2·0.26, measured 0.0548 in GEMSDOE32).

Thinning a thick fault surface to dotted Poisson disk (d2.8 = 280 m) removes mass already within the same kernel footprint — it was covered, so it contributed only FP cost. Thinning keeps geometry but raises credit per dot. The group measured this: solid→dotted 0.1280→0.1839 (+44%) with 60% fewer pixels (H25, GEMSDOE10).

Off-catalogue enforcement is pure budget reallocation. Staff confirmed known faults are masked from evaluation (forum 11516) — mass on catalogue earns nothing and costs nothing, so it is pure waste. The 0.2778 file deleted every dot within 2 px (200 m) of the catalogue (37,654 dots, 0 on-catalogue), reallocating budget to where hidden faults are inferred (blind lattice 0.0904 → truth density ≈0.24%, GEMSDOE24).

Can we beat 0.2778 / 0.3262?

Beating the public leader 0.3262 (ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED #1 nchuzhoy, 2026-10-04 snapshot) at the same emitted mass requires ≈25% more mean credit per dot than the group’s best field (gap 0.0662 over 0.26, GEMSDOE32). No public fault compilation supplies it: GDR QFaults v2 59,065 px has only 1 px beyond 300 m of the given catalogue; SGMC has 61,664 px beyond but median 15 px (verified locally 2026-10-04). The path is a better detector plus the two-round objective (hidden faults become Final Round truth after expert review).

This lane changes where mass goes, not just how it is packed. H74-1 does not re-pack the same scarp surface; it targets concealed basement-block boundaries under basin fill (thick cover, low surface slope) — the population a Quaternary surface catalogue by construction omits. That is the discriminator that can raise credit density.
