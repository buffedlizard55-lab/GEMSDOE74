Evidence before leaderboard claims.

GEMSDOE74 is being built as an auditable workflow for testing new geological hypotheses, validating them with a spatial hide-and-recover holdout, and preparing a unique GEMS GeoTIFF only when its evidence is complete.

The first priority is a safe, reliable path from research to a valid submission—not a fabricated file, a copied raster, or a model projection presented as a score.

The reported H33 score and the leaderboard highs in the initiating request are not backed by a submission-page receipt in this workspace; the related GEMSDOE32 archive labels its H33 live value as a projection and says no organizer score exists for its artifacts.

The archive is useful for method review, not as a source of a file to copy. Its H33 method is described as pruning predictions near the known-fault catalogue. A real score change, if confirmed, would not by itself establish a causal geological explanation. The score-claim review records the discrepancy and the sources used.

The path from hypothesis to a real submission

  1. Restore verified inputs and the shared template's cache, evaluator, and writer.
  2. Register a unique hypothesis and check the candidate surface against the registry before placement.
  3. Run the prescribed spatial holdout and single-feature leakage checks. Keep projections separate from holdout DTI.
  4. Generate through the shared writer, validate the GeoTIFF and duplicate guard, and archive the hashes and receipts.
  5. Only after passing every gate, expose a download, provide a unique competition name and short comment, and record the organizer receipt.
